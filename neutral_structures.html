Neutral Structures

Unclaimed buildings scattered across every map. Capture them and you hold a permanent advantage — for as long as you hold them.

These structures belong to no faction. They sit on the map at the start of every match, undefended and fully functional. The first engineer through the door hands their bonus to whatever flag he's carrying.

Economy

Tech Oil Derrick

Provides a constant trickle of credits to whoever controls it. Contested constantly; defended rarely.

Intelligence

Tech Radar Tower

Reveals a large area of the map on capture. What you do with the information is your problem.

Healing

Tech Field Hospital

Any infantry moving through the capture radius is restored to full health.

Defense
