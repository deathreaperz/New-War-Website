Stolen Tech Units

Capture the right structure on the battlefield and any commander — regardless of flag — can field these units.

Scattered across New War's maps are tech structures that, once captured, unlock hardware built from recovered or repurposed enemy equipment. None of it belongs to any one faction, and all of it is available to whoever holds the ground.
