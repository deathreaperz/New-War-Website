Case File 04 · Full Briefing

The war that rewrote itself

Everything the official record says happened after the Chrono Chamber fired — and everything it left out.

Timeline A · Sealed

What the record says

By the official account, the Third War ended the way every briefing said it would. Yuri's psychic empire collapsed. The Chrono Chamber was fired, his consciousness erased from a timeline that no longer had room for him. The Allied Nations and the Soviet Union stood down, exhausted, and the world moved on to rebuilding.

Part One

The extraction

The record is wrong. In the seconds before the Chamber fired, a detachment of Yuri's most loyal officers broke into the facility's containment ring and forced open a corridor into the Time Space itself — the same fold in reality Yuri had planned to use to rewrite history from the inside.

They got him out. What they didn't account for was the cost: pulling a mind out of a collapsing timeline doesn't erase the collapse. It just moves it somewhere else.

Part Two

The fracture

What followed wasn't a new timeline so much as a wound in the old one. Events that should have been settled — battles, treaties, the quiet years after a war — began resolving differently depending on who you asked. Soviet command has three separate accounts of the Chamber's activation. None of them agree, and all three are classified.

Field commanders on every side started reporting the same thing: terrain that didn't match the maps, units that remembered engagements that hadn't happened yet, and silence from Yuri command structures that should have gone dark years ago.

Part Three

What grew in the wound

A paradox left unresolved doesn't stay empty. Something is drawing power directly from the fracture itself — a force with no flag, no prior record, and technology that by any honest timeline shouldn't be buildable for another several decades. Field reports have started calling it Black Force, for lack of a better name.

It isn't allied with Yuri's remnants. It isn't allied with anyone. It simply arrived, fully formed, the moment the timeline broke — and it intends to make sure the break stays open.

Ongoing

Where this leaves the war

The Allied Nations and the Soviet Union are still fighting the war they think they're in. Yuri is rebuilding around a leader nobody has confirmed seeing. And Black Force is spending resources on something none of the three old powers understand yet.

A new future will be forged, or a new conqueror will be born. Do what you must.
