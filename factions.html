Order of Battle

Four powers, one fracture

Two old superpowers, one psychic remnant, and one force that shouldn't exist yet. Here's who's fighting through the broken timeline.

Faction One

Allied Nations

The coalition that won the last war is fighting to prove the win still counts. Allied doctrine leans on speed, precision, and directed-energy weapons that punish anyone who commits their forces carelessly — a style built for commanders who'd rather outmaneuver an enemy than outlast them.

In New War, the Allies field a rebuilt naval and air arsenal alongside veteran ground units, with new prototype technology developed in response to threats the original war never had to plan for.

DoctrinePrecision & mobility
StrengthAir & naval superiority
Signature techDirected-energy weapons

Faction Two

Soviet Union

The Union never fully stood down, and in New War that suspicion is vindicated. Soviet forces trade finesse for mass — heavier armor, harder-hitting artillery, and a production doctrine built on simply outbuilding whoever's in front of them.

A new generation of armored vehicles and siege weapons fills out the roster, alongside veterans of the original war whose loyalty to the flag never wavered even as the timeline around them did.

DoctrineMass & attrition
StrengthHeavy armor & artillery
Signature techOverwhelming firepower
Full Overview Full Roster
Faction Three

Yuri

What remains of Yuri's command structure never disbanded — it went quiet. Yuri operates from the shadows of the fracture itself, rebuilding a psychic arsenal around a leader no field commander has confirmed seeing since the extraction.

Mind control, battlefield manipulation, and unconventional infantry remain Yuri's calling card, now paired with new constructs pulled from the same instability that let him escape in the first place.

DoctrineControl & subversion
StrengthPsychic warfare
Signature techMind control & manipulation

Faction Four

Black Force

No flag, no prior record, no explanation the other three powers can agree on. Black Force arrived fully formed the moment the timeline fractured, wielding technology field analysts date to several decades in the future — if it can be dated at all.

Its forces bend the battlefield itself: units that phase in and out of engagements, structures that reposition without warning, and a doctrine that treats time as just another terrain feature to exploit.
