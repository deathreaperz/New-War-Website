Synopsis

What if the ending of Yuri's Revenge was a lie? What if Yuri's forces breached the Time Space itself to pull him out of the Chrono Chamber before it fired?

The extraction didn't just save him — it tore a hole through the Third War's own history. A paradox took root, and from it, a future nobody planned for began to grow. The Allied Nations and the Soviet Union still stand, still at each other's throats, but neither remembers a war that unfolds differently now. Something new grew in the wound the paradox left behind, and it answers to no old flag.

A new future will be forged, or a new conqueror will be born. Do what you must.
